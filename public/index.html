Image-Guided Bronchoscopy

Fraxinus is an open-source research platform for image-guided bronchoscopy, developed by SINTEF Medical Technology in collaboration with St. Olavs Hospital, Trondheim University Hospital, Norway. Built on top of CustusX, Fraxinus provides a streamlined workflow for virtual bronchoscopy planning, integrating CT imaging, airway segmentation, and route planning into an intuitive research tool.

Fraxinus enables researchers to import CT volumes, automatically segment the lung airways, and plan routes to targets via virtual bronchoscopy. The platform is designed for use in clinical research and has been applied in studies of minimally invasive lung diagnostics and therapy.

The software is freely available under a BSD-3 license. Fraxinus is a research tool and is not intended for routine clinical use. It is neither FDA nor CE approved.

About Fraxinus

Fraxinus is a planning and navigation software for research in bronchoscopy. It provides a smooth and simple workflow for virtual bronchoscopy built on top of the CustusX research platform.

The Fraxinus workflow guides the user through a sequence of steps: importing a CT volume, automatically segmenting the lung airways, setting a bronchoscopy target, and navigating to that target via a virtual fly-through of the airways. The platform is designed to be used by expert personnel in a clinical research setting.
